# Reference Website Audit — TeddyPups U.A.E.

## 1. Overview
- **Reference URL:** [https://puppy.instabizweb.com/](https://puppy.instabizweb.com/)
- **Platform Architecture:** Built originally using Next.js (App Router) with SSR and image optimization pipelines.
- **Brand Identity:** TeddyPups U.A.E. — A Dubai-based luxury pet brand specializing in miniature and teacup puppies (Maltipoo, Pomeranian, Toy Poodle, Cavapoo, Shih Tzu, and Korean Teacups).
- **Theme Concept:** Cozy, playful, and trustworthy aesthetic featuring custom scallop hem dividers, warm pastel colors, and handwritten scrawl accents.

---

## 2. Page & Navigation Inventory
Inspection of the reference website revealed 6 primary navigation destinations plus sub-routes:
1. **Home (`/`):** Hero section with peeking puppy lineup, "Meet the pups" card showcase, two adoption paths (Local Showroom vs Imported), journey timeline, trust badges, family testimonials preview, FAQ preview, and primary CTA.
2. **Available Puppies (`/puppies`):** Complete interactive listing of 15 currently available puppies with filters for origin (local/imported) and breed (Maltipoo, Pomeranian, Poodle, Cavapoo, Shih Tzu).
3. **Puppy Details (`/puppies/:slug`):** Individual puppy profile page featuring high-res photo gallery, gender, age, health clearance status, origin badge, narrative description, direct WhatsApp enquiry CTA, and related puppies.
4. **Gone Home (`/gone-home`):** Showcases previously adopted puppies and their new families across the UAE.
5. **Happy Families (`/happy-families`):** Wall of happy endings featuring client reviews, testimonial quotes, owner locations, and photo gallery of adopted puppies.
6. **How It Works (`/how-it-works`):** Comprehensive breakdown of the two paths (Local Adoption vs Overseas Import) including health testing, official passport/microchip guarantees, and delivery options (`#delivery`).
7. **Questions & Answers (`/faq`):** Full categorized accordion FAQ covering Health & Safety, Breeds & Lineage, Pricing, Showroom Visits, and Delivery.
8. **Visit & Contact (`/contact`):** Interactive contact form with validation, Downtown Dubai showroom address, opening hours, Google Maps location, and WhatsApp contact links.

## 3. Visual Design System Audit
- **Color Tokens:**
  - `milk`: `#fffdf8` (Primary background tone)
  - `cream`: `#fff1d8` (Secondary card background & section fill)
  - `honey`: `#fbe3b4` (Warm golden accent & badges)
  - `caramel`: `#f2be6b` (Highlight badge & warm tint)
  - `toffee`: `#e4a352` (Accent stroke & icons)
  - `cinnamon`: `#a24a1a` (Rich terracotta brand accent & subtitles)
  - `cocoa`: `#2a170e` (Primary typography and button fill)
  - `whatsapp`: `#25D366` (Official WhatsApp call-to-action green)
- **Typography:**
  - Display / Plush: Rounded, friendly sans-serif (`Nunito`, 800 weight)
  - Handwritten / Scrawl: Cursive casual accent (`Caveat`, 600 weight)
- **Decorative Elements:**
  - Scallop pattern border SVG hem for header and footer transitions
  - Cute paw SVG icons on navigation links and testimonial ratings
  - Arched top door-like frames for puppy photos
